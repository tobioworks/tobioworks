# Applied AI/ML Timeline — 2019–Present

This page is deliberately portfolio-safe. It summarizes selected AI/ML, data, product, and software work without naming employers, customers, collaborators, private repositories, proprietary datasets, credentials, or confidential implementation details.

It is also intentionally precise about maturity: research, discovery, architecture, and prototypes are not described as production deployments unless separately evidenced.

## 2019–2021 — Digital health platform and ML product exploration

**Problem space**  
Digitizing clinical workflows, remote monitoring, care pathways, data exchange, and decision support across healthcare environments.

**Selected work**
- Worked across product discovery, workflow design, software architecture, and technical validation for a digital-health platform.
- Designed and evaluated architecture patterns spanning enterprise data warehousing, analytics, microservices, integration engines, APIs, data transformation, data governance, and sensor/device connectivity.
- Explored federated-data and federated-AI approaches intended to reduce unnecessary movement of sensitive data while still enabling model improvement.
- Developed and evaluated concepts for clinical risk classification, remote monitoring, and AI-assisted diagnostic workflows.
- Worked with international stakeholders on onboarding flows, product demonstrations, pilots, and platform expansion opportunities.

**Maturity**  
Product discovery, architecture, research, and prototyping. No blanket production-deployment claim is made here.

## 2020–2021 — Life-sciences ML and medical imaging

**Problem space**  
Applying machine learning to clinical data, medical imaging, and life-sciences workflows where dataset quality, generalization, privacy, and clinical usefulness matter as much as model accuracy.

**Selected work**
- Ran recurring ML-focused work sessions covering life-sciences ML infrastructure, experimentation, genomics, and text summarization/search.
- Investigated medical-imaging ML use cases including histopathology classification, tissue discrimination, mammogram mass segmentation, lung imaging, assisted detection, and organ segmentation.
- Evaluated public imaging datasets and the practical gap between laboratory model accuracy and real-world clinical performance.
- Explored deep-learning and convolutional-network approaches for image classification and segmentation.
- Evaluated data-platform cost, architecture, and operational constraints alongside the model work rather than treating ML as an isolated notebook exercise.

**Maturity**  
Research, technical discovery, architecture, and prototype-oriented work.

## 2021–2023 — Applied ML, automation, and intelligent information processing

**Problem space**  
Using ML and automation to improve decision quality, information extraction, and operational workflows.

**Selected work**
- Explored ML approaches for improving diagnostic-test sensitivity and combining test, imaging, and contextual data into better decision-support workflows.
- Worked on concepts around secure data collection, API-based information exchange, scoring, triage, and privacy-aware data handling.
- Investigated intelligent document processing and workflow-automation opportunities for repetitive business processes.
- Continued evaluating language-model capabilities and their implications for product and workflow design as the technology matured.

**Maturity**  
Mixed product discovery, R&D, technical evaluation, and prototyping.

## 2025–Present — Agentic AI and enterprise operations

**Problem space**  
Moving from isolated model calls to dependable AI-enabled workflows that interact with enterprise systems and human decision points.

**Selected work**
- Built and evaluated tool-using agents and multi-step agentic workflows.
- Worked with retrieval, state/memory, evaluation, regression testing, evidence/provenance, approval boundaries, escalation, and human-in-the-loop controls.
- Applied AI-assisted software engineering to shorten the path from problem framing to working software.
- Prototyped and implemented automation patterns around ERP, commerce, APIs, documents, email, operational data, and approval workflows.
- Focused on observability, failure modes, governance, latency/cost trade-offs, and evidence quality rather than demo-only behavior.

**Maturity**  
A mix of working prototypes and operational automation. Public material intentionally omits confidential source code and implementation details.

- **Multi-year, project-based AI/ML experience** spanning classical ML, computer vision, data platforms, language models, and agentic systems.
- Ability to move between **product discovery, architecture, technical prototyping, evaluation, and operational workflow design**.

This timeline does **not** claim continuous full-time ML-engineering employment across the entire period. It records evidence-backed, project-based AI/ML work performed alongside broader product, software, platform, and transformation responsibilities.
