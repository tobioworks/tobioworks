# Evidence-Governed Media Intelligence — case study candidate

Status: portfolio-safe draft.

## Problem class
A recurring media-monitoring workflow needs broad source coverage, attributable records, compact decision support, and human editorial review.

## Product approach
Separate discovery, evidence capture, observations, clustering, end-user dossier, operational projection, and supervised feedback.

## What this demonstrates
Agentic product design, evidence/provenance, evaluation against human references, human-in-the-loop workflow, and failure-driven iteration.

## Disclosure boundary
No customer identities, private repositories, raw datasets, political targeting, credentials, or proprietary implementation details.
